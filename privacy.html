Privacy Policy

About this policy

This page explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you browse this website or contact Caravantastic about a stay. Please review and customise this policy to reflect the business’s actual practices before relying on it.

Information you provide

If you contact us or submit a booking enquiry, you may provide details such as your name, contact information, requested dates and lodge preference. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage booking requests, communicate about a potential stay and meet applicable legal obligations. Booking requests are subject to confirmation.

Storage, sharing and your choices

Information should be retained only as needed and shared only where appropriate to handle your enquiry, provide a service or meet legal requirements. You may contact Caravantastic to ask about information you have provided or raise a privacy concern.

This is a general draft and does not confirm the website’s actual data practices, cookies, analytics or third-party services. Update it with accurate business contact details and practices before publication.
