Room description

Located at our beautiful Windermere Tranquil Retreat property, The Elysian Suite is on the first floor and comes with a private car parking space.

Please note: this property is just under half a mile’s drive from Aphrodites, where you check in. Directions will be given at check-in.

This suite boasts a balcony with an outdoor hot tub for your own personal use, a Swedish sauna, a feature bath and a large cinema TV.
