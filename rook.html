About this treehouse

Nestled deep in the Herefordshire countryside, The Rook’s Nook is a magical, romantic treehouse retreat designed for couples seeking peace, privacy, and a touch of woodland enchantment. This dog-friendly treehouse cabin blends rustic charm with luxurious details, featuring ornate Persian curtains, a beautifully handcrafted cosy bed, and an atmosphere that feels straight out of a fairytale.

Built around a living tree that rises through the balcony, this unique hideaway immerses you completely in nature. Surrounded by tranquil woodland, birdsong, and wildlife, it’s the perfect place to slow down, reconnect, and enjoy uninterrupted seclusion.

Step onto your private balcony and dine among the treetops. As the sun sets, watch the sky turn golden, then unwind beneath a blanket of stars. Drift off to sleep to the gentle sounds of owls and rustling leaves — the essence of a romantic Herefordshire getaway.

For pure indulgence, your secluded garden features a luxurious private hot tub, complete with its own projector screen, and a roll-top bath, softly lit with fairy lights and candles for dreamy evenings outdoors. Enhance your stay with a rejuvenating infra-red sauna and an invigorating cold splash bucket — creating your own boutique spa experience in the heart of the woods. And don’t forget the secret cinema hidden underneath the cabin.

Enjoy relaxed outdoor dining with your own BBQ and pizza oven, perfect for long, romantic evenings together. Snuggle up beside the chiminea and watch the stars reveal themselves.

Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, planning a weekend escape, or simply craving time together in nature, The Rook’s Nook is a romantic treehouse escape designed to make every moment unforgettable.
